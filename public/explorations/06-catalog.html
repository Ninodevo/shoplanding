FAQ

The questions buyers ask before paying.

Is this a page builder?
No. The block order is opinionated and fixed — only content and visual tokens change between themes. Every block exists for a documented reason; rearranging silently breaks rule coverage.
Will this work for my niche?
Yes for any single-product launch where the customer is making one decision. We ship niche presets so the defaults match the category.
How is this different from a Theme Store theme?
Theme Store themes position on visuals. ShopLanding positions on conversion logic. The marketing site you're reading is the proof — every section is one of the blocks we sell, traceable to a documented rule.
Can my agency rebuild on a different stack?
Yes. The unlimited license includes a portable system spec (JSON + Markdown) so a competent team can reimplement on Hydrogen, Astro, Webflow, or anywhere else.
Do I get updates after I buy?
Lifetime updates for the stores covered by your license. No subscription, no upgrade cycle. New niche presets land for free.
Refund policy?
14 days, no questions, on the digital license. Setup add-on refundable until work begins.
